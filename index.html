👋 About Me

Hello! I'm Julien, a passionate fullstack developer with a love for clean, functional code and beautiful user experiences. I believe in the power of simplicity and the elegance of well-crafted solutions.

I have a preference for functional programming with Clojure. I'm drawn to its thoughtful design, which makes it incredibly versatile for a wide range of tasks, and its seamless bridge to the vast JVM and Javascript ecosystems.

I specialize in creating, maintaining, and scaling complex systems with my Frontend, Backend, and DevOps knowledge. I love to bring all the pieces together to create products that customers want 🚀

Outside of work, I'm an avid reader, always seeking to expand my thinking and deepen my knowledge.

I'm currently based in Amsterdam, but I'm open to remote opportunities.
